About the Artist

Hello, I'm Jane.

I paint to slow down and pay attention — to the way light moves across a hillside, the alertness in a wild animal's eyes, the structure hidden inside a single petal.

My journey into painting began over fifteen years ago, sketching birds in the garden of my childhood home. What started as a way to notice the world more closely grew into a full-time practice in oil and watercolor, focused entirely on the landscapes, animals and plants I encounter on long walks near my studio.

Each painting can take anywhere from two weeks to several months, built up in thin layers to capture the realism I look for — the texture of fur, the translucence of a leaf held up to the sun, the haze of distance in a valley. My goal is always the same: to make you feel like you could step into the scene.

Today my work is held in private collections across North America, Europe and Asia, and I continue to paint from my studio, taking on select commissions each year.
